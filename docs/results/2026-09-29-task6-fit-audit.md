## Task 6 decision

The engineering fit objective is achieved for the approved TinyStories-1M target: the compiler-generated design mapped, legally routed, produced a bitstream, and executed exact full-model autoregressive inference on the board. Stop the strategy search; neither five additional strategies nor DDR3 inference is needed. **The Task 6 publication/reproducibility deliverables are not yet closed**: publish the strategy comparison and restore or explicitly qualify the missing exact build-source closure. Task 4's interface/demo package and Task 5's scaling matrix remain separate work.
