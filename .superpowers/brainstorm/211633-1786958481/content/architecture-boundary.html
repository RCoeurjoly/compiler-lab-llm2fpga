Boundary decisions
  • The generic MLIR/CIRCT pipeline is preserved as evidence, not used on the completion path.
  • The fork owns upstream-derived accelerator RTL; LLM2FPGA owns packaging, generation, integration, evaluation, and reproducible builds.
  • The accelerator—not the host—executes the complete 16-token autoregressive loop.
  • JTAG is diagnostic only; accepted demonstrations use PCIe.
