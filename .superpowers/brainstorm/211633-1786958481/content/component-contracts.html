Isolation rules
  • No model-specific source edits: differences enter through packages and generator parameters.
  • Every artifact is content-addressed; a result names the exact checkpoint, package, RTL closure, tools, and bitstream.
  • On-chip and DDR3 modes share compute semantics and the PCIe ABI; only the storage service changes.
  • A stage failure produces a durable diagnostic artifact instead of an absent result.
