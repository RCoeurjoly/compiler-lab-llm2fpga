#+TITLE: Task 6 — TinyStories-1M resource reduction and FPGA fit
#+DATE: 2026-09-29

* Scope and decision

Task 3 reported approximately 143x resource overutilization. Task 6's
engineering objective is to obtain a functionally valid compiler-generated
TinyStories-1M implementation that fits the YPCB Kintex-7 FPGA. The measured
on-chip candidate has now legally routed and run the authenticated full
16-token continuation on the board. Its fit evidence and limitations are
audited in [[file:docs/results/2026-09-29-task6-fit-audit.md][the Task 6 fit audit]]. Stop the strategy search here; a campaign of five
more experiments would not advance the agreed objective.

This is an explicit revision of [[file:docs/project-plan_v2.org][project-plan_v2.org]]: successful fit permits
stopping before its 5–10-strategy minimum, and TinyStories-1M is the Task 6
fit target even if Task 5 later lowers a larger model. The original
deliverables remain a survey, usage/reproduction references, and a
before/after comparison. This report does not assert that fit alone proves
Task 4 board-inference packaging or full timing qualification.

* Conclusion

The measured full-model system fits, legally routes and performs exact
on-board autoregressive inference, so Task 6 is complete under the approved
TinyStories-1M baseline and early-stop rule. The ROM-mapping ablation is the
defensible quantitative single-variable resource result. The numerical and
shared-datapath contributions are real parts of the accepted system but lack
isolated deltas. DDR3 externalization was investigated and deferred. The
committed Nix recipe reproduces the accepted FPGA configuration without
relying on historical ad hoc store derivations. It does not establish full
BRAM/DSP timing coverage or constitute a new hardware run. NLnet has not
accepted the user's deviations from the original project-plan wording.
